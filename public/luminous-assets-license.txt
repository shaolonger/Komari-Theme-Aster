Changes: rendered on a rotating WebGL sphere, combined with lighting, city lights,
clouds, a blue color grade and an atmospheric rim. The original local texture
files are distributed with this theme. The provider describes its maps as based
on NASA elevation and imagery data.

The previous luminous-earth.svg outline is derived from Natural Earth 1:110m,
public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.

The four luminous-*-v2.png server/city illustrations were generated with OpenAI
ImageGen for this theme from the user's visual references. They are decorative;
locations and connection arcs do not represent measured network routes.
